The Billiards Room

CosmoPolitan Club's Billiards Room is a sanctuary for precision and strategy, housing professional-standard billiards and snooker tables in an atmosphere of quiet dignity and classic club character.

The room features premium tournament-grade tables maintained to international standards, expert cue racks, and a relaxed seating area for spectators — the ideal setting for friendly matches and serious competition alike.

  • Professional full-size billiards tables
  • Tournament-grade snooker table
  • Expert cue selection and accessories
  • Dedicated coaching sessions available
  • Annual Billiards Championship hosted
