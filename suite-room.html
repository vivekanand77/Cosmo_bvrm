Suite Room

Luxury Living Space — Spacious Layout & Separate Living Area

Guest Rooms

Refined Luxury

The Suite Rooms at CosmoPolitan Club offer an elevated level of luxury and space. Perfect for families or members seeking premium comfort, each suite features a separate living area, offering privacy and a spacious layout.

Decorated with rich wooden finishes and plush textiles, our suites are designed to make you feel right at home. Guests can enjoy panoramic views of the club's lush gardens and sports grounds.

  • Separate living room with sofa set & coffee table
  • King-size bed with premium pillow-top mattress
  • Two flat-screen TVs (43" in bedroom, 50" in living room)
  • Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi access
  • Mini-refrigerator and personal bar counter
  • Luxury bathroom with premium tub and amenities
