Secretariat Office

The Secretariat office is the administrative backbone of the CosmoPolitan Club, managing day-to-day operations, member relations, billing, facilities booking, and overall club governance support.

Our dedicated administrative staff is committed to providing efficient and friendly service to all members, ensuring that your club experience is seamless and enjoyable.

  • Membership applications and documentation processing
  • Billing, subscription payments, and account services
  • Room reservations, banquet hall, and auditorium bookings
  • Feedback management and member support services
  • Coordination of official club communications and events
