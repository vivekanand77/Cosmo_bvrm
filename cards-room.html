The Cards Room

The Cards Room at CosmoPolitan Club offers a refined and convivial atmosphere for members to enjoy a range of card games, board games and indoor recreation in excellent company.

Featuring elegant furnishings, comfortable seating and a dedicated service area, the room is the ideal venue for an afternoon of rummy, bridge, chess or casual social gaming with fellow members.

  • Dedicated card and board game tables
  • Rummy, bridge and chess available
  • Comfortable lounge seating
  • In-room refreshment service
  • Regular member tournaments hosted
