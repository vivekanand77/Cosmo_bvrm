Club Membership

Categories of Members

Category A

Founder Members

All those members who have signed on the Memorandum of Association shall be the founder members of the Club.

Category B

Donor Members

The club may accept donations to a minimum of two times of the subscription of the life member. All such members will be donors of the club.

Category C

Life Members

The membership in this category remains active during the entire lifetime of the enrolled member.

Category D

Honorary Membership

The Executive Body may induct any person as Chief Patron. However, he will remain as an honorary member of the club. His membership is restricted only to those with distinction and whose services are considered necessary to promote the interest of the club.

Category E

Senior Citizen Membership

The Executive Body may give senior citizen membership to retired gazetted officers whose services are considered necessary to promote the interest of the club.

Category F

Temporary Membership

Any person may be enrolled as a temporary member of the Club for a period not exceeding 3 months in any calendar year.

Category G

Corporate Membership

The Executive Body may give corporate membership to any Company incorporated under the companies act, body corporate, renowned industry, or association (not including a cooperative society). Such Company may nominate a minimum of 2 and maximum of 4 representatives based on the Membership fee paid.
