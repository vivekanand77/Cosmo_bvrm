Indoor Auditorium

The CosmoPolitan Club Indoor Auditorium is a state-of-the-art 500-seat hall equipped with professional acoustic panels, a fully-rigged stage, and audio-visual systems for cultural programmes, screenings, lectures and private events.

Available for member bookings and club events, the auditorium has hosted some of the most prestigious cultural evenings, annual day celebrations and distinguished speaker talks in the region.

  • 500-seat acoustically designed hall
  • Professional stage with curtains and lighting rigs
  • High-definition projection and sound system
  • Air-conditioned with climate control
  • Green room and backstage facilities
  • Available for private event bookings
