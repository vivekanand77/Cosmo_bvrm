Maharaja Suite

Royal Accommodation — Teakwood Furnishings, Jacuzzi & Private Lounge

Guest Rooms

Royal Opulence

The Maharaja Suite Room is the pinnacle of opulent hospitality at the CosmoPolitan Club. True to its royal name, this expansive suite is decorated with classic handcrafted teakwood furniture, traditional carpets, and rich royal colors.

Featuring an independent dining room, a personal lounge, and a master bedroom with a jacuzzi bathtub, the Maharaja Suite is tailored for prestigious stays and VIP visits.

  • Royal master bedroom with four-poster king bed
  • Independent private dining area & lounge
  • Jacuzzi bathtub and premium luxury bath amenities
  • Two large flat-screen Smart TVs (55" & 65")
  • Dedicated butler service on request
  • Private balcony overlooking the club lawns
