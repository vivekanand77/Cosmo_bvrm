The Walking Track

The CosmoPolitan Club features a premium 400-metre rubberized walking and jogging track designed to offer a safe, low-impact surface for your daily fitness routines.

Surrounded by beautifully landscaped gardens, mature shade trees, and resting benches, the track offers a tranquil environment for morning walks, evening runs, and active recreation.

  • 400-metre professional rubberized jogging loop
  • Lush green landscaping and peaceful surroundings
  • Ample lighting for safe evening walks
  • Resting benches and water stations along the track
  • Exclusively open to members and their registered guests
