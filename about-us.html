Our Story

CosmoPolitan Club,
Bhimavaram

CosmoPolitan Club is situated at Suryanarayana Puram, Bhimavaram, West Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh. Located in the heart of Bhimavaram, the club stands as the premier social and athletic hub for prominent citizens, professionals, and families of the region.

Established with a vision to foster community fellowship, sportsmanship, and cultural enrichment, the club provides an exclusive sanctuary featuring world-class indoor and outdoor sporting arenas, executive guest rooms, fine dining restaurants, and lush green walking tracks.

CosmoPolitan Club is dedicated to encouraging athletic excellence across Tennis, Badminton, Table Tennis, Swimming, Billiards, and Fitness, while hosting year-round cultural festivals, tournaments and social gatherings for all member families.
