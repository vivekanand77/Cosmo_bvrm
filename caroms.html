The Caroms Room

The CosmoPolitan Club Caroms Room provides a comfortable and convivial space for members to enjoy caroms, chess and other board games in a relaxed club atmosphere.

With quality tournament-grade carom boards and a dedicated seating area, the room hosts regular inter-member tournaments and is a popular gathering spot throughout the day.

  • 6 tournament-grade carom boards
  • Chess and other board games available
  • Comfortable lounge seating
  • In-room refreshment service
  • Regular member tournaments hosted
