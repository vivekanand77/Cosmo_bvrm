Premier Tennis Courts

CosmoPolitan Club's tennis facilities feature professional-grade synthetic and clay courts equipped with high-lumen floodlights for day and evening play throughout the year.

With certified coaching staff, well-maintained court surfaces and an active tournaments calendar, the tennis programme is one of the flagship sporting offerings of the club.

  • 4 full-size synthetic courts with floodlighting
  • 2 clay courts for clay-court specialists
  • AITA-certified coaching available
  • Annual Club Championship tournament
  • Equipment rental and pro-shop facilities
