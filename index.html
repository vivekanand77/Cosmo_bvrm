CosmoPolitan Club is situated at Suryanarayana Puram, Bhimavaram, West Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh, India. Located in the heart of Bhimavaram, the club stands as the premier social and athletic hub for prominent citizens, professionals, and families of the region.

Established with a vision to foster community fellowship, sportsmanship, and cultural enrichment, the club provides an exclusive sanctuary featuring world-class indoor and outdoor sporting arenas, executive guest rooms, fine dining restaurants, and lush green walking tracks.

CosmoPolitan Club is dedicated to encouraging athletic excellence across Tennis, Badminton, Table Tennis, Swimming, Billiards, and Fitness, while hosting year-round cultural festivals, tournaments, and social gatherings for all member families.

Est. Bhimavaram, Andhra Pradesh

CosmoPolitan Club is more than a membership — it is a way of life. Since its founding, the club has served as the social and sporting heart of Bhimavaram, bringing together families, professionals and community leaders under one distinguished roof.

From championship-level sports courts and a state-of-the-art fitness centre to fine dining, cultural events and luxurious guest accommodations — every aspect of the club is designed to enrich the lives of its members.

Signature Venue

Grand Banquet Hall

The CosmoPolitan Club's Grand Banquet Hall is the premier venue for weddings, receptions, corporate galas and cultural celebrations in Bhimavaram. Accommodating large gatherings with elegance, the hall features exceptional acoustics, ambient lighting and in-house catering.

With professional event management support and a dedicated team, every occasion hosted at the club becomes a cherished memory for members and their guests.
