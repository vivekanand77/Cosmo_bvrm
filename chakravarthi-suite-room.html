Presidential Grandeur

The Chakravarthi Suite Room is our ultimate Presidential Suite, designed for dignitaries, VIPs, and those seeking the finest accommodations. This majestic suite offers an unmatched level of grandeur, state-of-the-art facilities, and absolute privacy.

It includes a grand master bedroom, an expansive living and conference room, a private kitchenette, and a luxury bathroom featuring a premium bathtub, rain shower, and dedicated dressing area.

  • Dignitary-grade master bedroom with imperial king bed
  • Expansive living room with integrated conference table
  • Private kitchenette with refrigerator & coffee station
  • Luxury bath with dressing area, bathtub & rain shower
  • Premium Smart TVs (55" & 75") with sound system
  • Private entrance and security lobby
