Constitution & Bylaws — Guidelines for Members & Guests

19

Golden Rules
of Club Conduct

Constitution & Bylaws of CosmoPolitan Club

1. General & Operating Hours

The Club remains open daily for members and eligible guests. Playing Hours are designated for sporting activities, while Club Hours refer to general operating times for services as determined by the Committee.

Definitions

Guest: A non-member introduced by a member.

Playing Guest: Invited to participate in sports on general playing days.

2. Playing Days & Fair Use

Playing facilities are available on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays. Members must use facilities fairly: no player or group may monopolize shuttle courts, tennis courts, table tennis tables, or billiards tables when others are waiting. vacate courts immediately after set completion.

Fair Use Court Rule

Singles games should not be started in tennis or shuttle if another player is waiting and willing to join doubles play.

3. Swimming Pool Rules

Proper swimming attire is compulsory for all entering the pool, and users must take a shower beforehand. All users must adhere strictly to hygiene and pool safety standards.

Pool Timings

Morning: 5:00 AM – 8:30 AM

Evening: 4:00 PM – 8:30 PM

Closed every Monday

4. Open-Air Theatre

A movie screening will be organized regularly for entertainment. Only Club members and their family members are permitted to attend the screenings.

Screening Details

Days: 2nd & 4th Sunday

Time: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

5. Walking Track & Outdoors

The Club provides outdoor facilities for members' fitness and recreational games. All outdoor facilities are available according to established schedules.

Outdoor Assets

• 250-Metre Walking Track

• Volleyball Court

• Cricket Pitch

6. Children's Park

The Children's Park is intended exclusively for the recreation and enjoyment of members' children. Parents and guardians are responsible for ensuring appropriate and safe use.

Play Equipment

Swings, see-saws, slides, towers, merry-go-round, and other children's play setups.

7. Student Members

Student members are entitled to use the playing facilities available to regular playing members, subject to the rules and schedules established by the Committee.

Time Limit

Standard playing time is limited to 2 hours. Extension requires court vacancy & Committee permission.

8. Guest Admission Rules

Members introducing guests must sign the Guest Register (available with the Bearer on duty) with the guest's full name and details. A guest or members' children are not permitted to introduce other guests.

Liability

The introducing member is fully responsible for the conduct and charges of their guest while on Club premises.

9 & 10. Indoor Games & Gymnasium

Indoor games are available throughout the year subject to timings. Gymnasium access is free of charge to Club members, while guests are subject to guest charges.

Gym Hours

Morning: 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM

Evening: 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM

11 & 12. Cards Room & Bar Rules

Card games must be played only in designated Rummy rooms. The bar is available for members' convenience. Children under 16 years are not permitted in or around the Bar area.

Timings

Cards Room: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Bar: 11:00 AM – 11:00 PM

Last order for drinks: 10:30 PM

13 & 14. Dress Code & Outside Food

Casual attire is permitted within the Club premises, but appropriate wear is expected for specific facilities. Members and guests are strictly not permitted to bring outside food/beverages for consumption inside the Club.

Quick Rule

All food and drinks must be ordered from the Club's authorized catering facilities.

15 & 16. Catering, Kitchen & Party Booking

Kitchen services are available for snacks and meals. For large parties, members must submit an application to the Secretary at least 3 days in advance. Approval is subject to Committee discretion.

Kitchen Schedule

Kitchen: 7:00 AM – 10:30 PM

Last Food Order: 10:00 PM

Party Orders: 20-25 pax (1 day notice); 100+ pax (3 days notice).

17, 18 & 19. Utilities, Property Damage & Suggestions

Members using landline facilities will be charged. Any loss/damage to Club property caused by members or guests must be compensated. Members are encouraged to record suggestions in the Suggestion Book at the secretariat.

Compliance

Damages are billed at actual repair or replacement cost as determined by the Committee.

General Compliance

All members, family members, and guests are expected to maintain discipline, respect Club property, and behave responsibly while using Club facilities.

The Committee reserves the right to modify, update, interpret, or enforce these rules and regulations whenever necessary in the interest of the Club. Failure to comply may result in appropriate action as determined by the Committee.

Members are requested to cooperate in maintaining a safe, respectful, clean, and enjoyable environment for everyone.

Inquiries
