Cosmo Sports Trust

The Cosmo Sports Trust was founded with the mission to promote sporting excellence in the region by sponsoring promising young athletes, improving local sports infrastructure, and organizing regional tournaments.

Through charity initiatives and membership contributions, the Trust funds sports training, coaching camps, equipment, and travel grants for state and national-level competitors.

  • Scholarships and financial support for young athletes
  • Funding for advanced coaching clinics and camps
  • Development of state-of-the-art sports facilities
  • Organization of annual inter-state youth championships
  • Partnerships with schools and sports associations

Key Focus Areas

Talent Sponsorship

Identifying and supporting under-privileged junior athletes with high potential, providing professional gear and training.

Infrastructure Funding

Upgrading regional courts, tracks, and equipment to ensure athletes train on international-standard surfaces.

Tournament Hosting

Organizing local and state-level leagues to give young players exposure to competitive arenas and scouts.
